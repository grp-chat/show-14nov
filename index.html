05 ARRIVAL TIME & INITIAL REHEARSAL

Preferred Arrival Time: 4:00 PM

We strongly encourage all participants to arrive by 4:00 PM for an initial rehearsal before the performance.

This will allow the children to:

  • Familiarise themselves with the performance arrangements.
  • Practise their event sequences and relay handovers.
  • Understand where to stand and when to get ready.
  • Make any necessary last-minute adjustments.

For Those Unable to Arrive at 4:00 PM

No worries! Please arrive as early as possible and inform Coach Andrew in advance. We will help you catch up with the necessary arrangements.

06 FOOD & MAKE-UP ARRANGEMENTS

5:30 PM – Food Provided by School

  • Food provided by the school is expected to be ready at 5:30 PM.
  • Children will have their food before proceeding with make-up.
  • After eating, the children will take turns going for make-up by the teacher.
  • Please arrive with a clean face and avoid applying make-up beforehand.
07 FINAL REHEARSAL BEFORE THE LIVE PERFORMANCE

When Coach Andrew arrives at Puay Chai, if time permits, we will have one final rehearsal before proceeding with the live performance.

  • All children, volunteer parents and the assistant coach should be ready and available when Coach Andrew arrives.
  • Please be prepared to go through the final performance arrangements, including event sequences, relay handovers and stage arrangements.
  • We will use this opportunity to make sure everyone understands their respective responsibilities before the actual performance begins.
Important: Please do not wait until Coach Andrew arrives to start getting ready. Everyone should be prepared in advance so that we can make full use of the remaining time.
08 STACKING EQUIPMENT ARRANGEMENT

For the Actual Performance

Only TWO sets of stacking equipment will be used throughout the actual performance.

The two designated sets will tentatively belong to:

  • TYY
  • MIN
Important: Participants must remember whose cups are designated for the actual performance. The children themselves must remember to bring these two sets onto the stage when required.

For Warming Up

All other participants must still bring their own stacking equipment.

Their personal equipment will be used for warming up and practice before the performance.

09 MEETING POINT & CLASSROOM ARRANGEMENTS

Our Designated Room: CLASSROOM 3A

Classroom 3A will be our main meeting point throughout the evening.

This is where we will:

  • Gather upon arrival.
  • Conduct our initial rehearsal.
  • Have our food.
  • Wait for our turns to go for make-up.

Please remain in Classroom 3A and wait for your turn to be arranged for make-up by the teacher.

Important Reminders for Volunteer Parents

We would appreciate our volunteer parents' help in keeping an eye on the children throughout the evening.

Toilet Breaks

  • Please ensure children return promptly after using the toilet.
  • Kindly discourage children from using toilet breaks as an excuse to wander around the school or disappear from the group.

Children Watching the Performance on the Projector

  • Children who choose to go downstairs to watch the projector must remain in the designated viewing area.
  • They must not wander around elsewhere.
  • If they need to leave the viewing area, please inform a volunteer parent.
  • Coach Andrew may need to locate them urgently when their events are coming up.
Important: We need to know where every child is at all times so that nobody goes missing when it is time to perform!

FOR VOLUNTEER PARENTS ONLY

The following two sections contain additional duties and arrangements specifically for volunteer parents. Parents who are not volunteering may skip these sections.

11 VOLUNTEER PARENTS' DUTIES & ARRANGEMENTS

We sincerely appreciate the help and cooperation of our volunteer parents. Your assistance will be very important in ensuring that everything runs smoothly throughout the performance.

1.

Line Judges & Stage Assistance

  • Volunteer parents are welcome to be on stage as line judges and help manage the children's performances.
  • Line judges can also help ensure that children waiting backstage remain seated and do not dance or move around unnecessarily, as this happened during the rehearsal.
  • If possible, line judges are encouraged to wear the same colour for a more coordinated appearance. This is not compulsory.

2. Live Score Updates

  • One volunteer parent will be in charge of updating the scores on the live scoreboard displayed on the digital backdrop.
  • This person will need to keep the scores updated throughout the performance.

3. Tournament Display Setup

  • Two volunteer parents will be in charge of setting up the tournament display before the performance.
  • They must ensure that the display is positioned correctly.
  • The assistant coach can help check and confirm the positioning.

4. Stage Table Arrangement

  • At 3:30 PM tomorrow, Coach Andrew will make sure the stage marker is in place.
  • Volunteer parents can then help ensure that the tables are positioned correctly according to the marker.
  • Please ensure that the tables are properly aligned before the children begin their performances.

5. Designated Stacking Equipment

  • A volunteer parent can help ensure that TYY's and MIN's designated stacking equipment is brought onto the stage when required.
  • Please help remind the children to bring the correct equipment onto the stage at the appropriate time.

6. Backstage Coordination & Display Setup

  • A volunteer parent can help check with the backstage team regarding the performance arrangements and readiness.
  • Please help ensure that we have sufficient time to set up the tournament display before our performance begins.
  • Keep track of the backstage situation and inform Coach Andrew if there are any updates or potential delays.
  • If necessary, help coordinate with the assistant coach to ensure the display is properly set up and ready before the children go on stage.
12 LIVE PERFORMANCE UPDATES

Assistance from Volunteer Parents / Assistant Coach

We would appreciate it if one of the volunteer parents or the assistant coach could help keep track of the performance progress at Puay Chai.

  • Monitor the performance flow and keep track of which events have been completed and which are coming up.
  • Update Coach Andrew regularly via WhatsApp on the actual progress.
  • Inform Coach Andrew immediately if there are any delays or changes to the performance schedule.

Why Is This Important?

As Coach Andrew will be travelling from SJK(C) Subang to SJK(C) Puay Chai, live updates will help him estimate how much time he has while travelling.

In fact, if there are delays at Puay Chai, that's actually a good thing for Coach Andrew! 😂

It means he has a little more breathing room and doesn't have to rush back like crazy from Subang.

Hopefully, everything goes smoothly, but a little extra time would certainly be helpful!
